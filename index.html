Invoice series check

GST invoice serial numbers must be consecutive, unique for a financial year and within the statutory character limit. MedBill checks the generated serial length. Confirm the actual series with the shop's GST practitioner before live issue.

Compliance boundary

MedBill is a billing tool, not a GST filing or drug-licensing system. Verify each medicine's current HSN and GST rate, supplier/customer status, invoice particulars, record retention and applicable pharmacy requirements with qualified professionals. This release does not generate e-invoices or e-way bills.
